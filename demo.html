“Floral” redirects here. For other uses,see Flower (disambiguation) and Floral (disambiguation).

Flowers,also known as blossoms and blooms, are the reproductive structures of flowering plants.

Typically, they are structured in four circular levels around the end of a stalk. These include: sepals, which are modified leaves that support the flower; petals, often designed to attract pollinators; male stamens, where pollen is presented; and female gynoecia, where pollen is received and its movement is facilitated to the egg. When flowers are arranged in a group, they are known collectively as an inflorescence.
